## 2. Understanding Canva Pro Text Sliders

Canva Pro uses two numerical sliders under the **Spacing** toolbar button (`≡↕`):

### 1. Letter Spacing Slider (-50 to 500)
- **`+10` to `+15`**: Standard body text in `Rajdhani Medium`. Gives the squared letters optimal legibility.
- **`+20` to `+25`**: Headings and titles in `Khand` and `Rajdhani`.
- **`+30` to `+45`**: All-Caps badges, document numbers (e.g. `REF: GES/2026/Q-142`), overlines, and column headers. Gives an engineered, blueprint look.

### 2. Line Spacing Slider (0.5 to 2.5)
- **`1.05` to `1.15`**: Big display titles in `Khand Bold`. Keeps multi-line titles tight and impactful without excessive gap.
- **`1.20` to `1.25`**: Subheadings, table headers, and bullet lists in `Rajdhani`.
- **`1.35` to `1.45`**: Body prose, formal paragraphs, and terms in `Rajdhani Medium`.

## 4. Multilingual Devanagari (Hindi & Marathi) in Canva

Both **Khand** and **Rajdhani** were created by the **Indian Type Foundry (ITF)** and include native Devanagari character sets. They align uniformly with English text:

- **When designing Marathi/Hindi Headlines**: Use **`Khand Bold`** (e.g. *शासकीय अधिकृत विद्युत व लिफ्ट कंत्राटदार*, *१३ मेगावॅट टर्नकी एक्सप्रेस फीडर*).
- **When designing Marathi/Hindi Paragraphs**: Use **`Rajdhani Medium`** or **`Rajdhani SemiBold`** (e.g. *भिवंडी, वाडा, कुडूस व ठाणे औद्योगिक क्षेत्रासाठी अखंड वीज सोल्यूशन्स*).
- **Numbers in Marathi/Hindi**: Both Arabic (`13 MW`, `22kV`) and Devanagari (`१३ मेगावॅट`, `२२केव्ही`) numbers maintain identical optical thickness in Khand Bold.

## 5. Canva Pro Do's and Don'ts

| Do's (Always Follow) | Don'ts (Strictly Avoid) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DO** use `Khand Bold` for high-impact numbers (`13 MW`, `₹ 45,00,000`, `20+ Years`). | **DON'T** use random decorative or script fonts (Brush, Comic, Pacifico). |
| **DO** use `Rajdhani` for readable body paragraphs and technical specifications. | **DON'T** use `Khand` for long body paragraphs (its condensed nature makes small body text hard to read). |
| **DO** stick to the exact Brand Navy (`#0D1392`) and Brand Orange (`#F69024`). | **DON'T** use generic bright red, neon orange, or plain web blue (`#0000FF`). |
| **DO** use Letter Spacing `+25` to `+40` on All-Caps badges and document numbers. | **DON'T** squish or horizontally stretch font boxes in Canva using side handles. |
| **DO** align all text left-aligned or cleanly justified in technical quotations. | **DON'T** center-align long technical paragraphs or BOQ specifications. |
