Frequently Asked Questions

Key insights regarding statutory approvals, D.G. set clearances, utility liaisons, and emergency electrical services.

What statutory approvals are required from the Electrical Inspector (PWD) for HT installations and D.G. sets? +

Under Regulation 43 of the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Safety Regulations and Section 54 of the Electricity Act, any electrical installation operating at High Tension (11kV, 22kV, 33kV) as well as Diesel Generator (D.G.) sets above statutory capacities require formal drawing scrutiny, earth pit resistance testing, interlock validation, and on-site physical inspection by the Electrical Inspectorate (PWD) before energization. Gayatri Electrical Services manages end-to-end liaisoning, submits Form A / Form B, and secures official charging permissions.

What HT and LT electrical contracting services do you provide under Torrent Power and MSEDCL supply circles? +

As a licensed Govt. Authorized Electrical Contractor, we execute turnkey HT and LT contracting across Torrent Power (Bhiwandi Circle) and MSEDCL (Mahavitaran). Our contracting scope includes new HT/LT industrial service connections, contract demand enhancement and reduction, dedicated overhead and underground HT cable laying, transformer and substation erection, Express Feeder commissioning, and CT/PT metering cubicle testing and charging approvals.

Which manufacturing and process industries do you provide electrical infrastructure for? +

We provide specialized HT/LT power engineering for diverse continuous manufacturing sectors, including:

  • Glass & Toughened Processing: Toughened (Tuff) glass tempering furnaces, continuous annealing lehrs, and quench blower power systems.
  • Printing & Packaging: High-speed printing presses, flexible tube laminates (e.g., EPL / Essel Propack), and tension drive power controls.
  • Irrigation & Agricultural Piping: Continuous PVC, HDPE, and drip agricultural pipe extrusion plants (e.g., Neer Irrigation).
  • Rubber & Polymer Manufacturing: Rubber moulding presses, vulcanization ovens, Banbury mixers, and extruders.
  • Office Furniture & Component Makers: Office chair fabrication, caster wheel moulding, and powder coating assembly lines.
  • Chemical & Pharmaceutical Plants: Flameproof (FLP) installations, reactors, and bulk drug units (e.g., Polypharm).
  • Warehousing & Cold Storage: Multi-tier logistics parks and multi-stage ammonia/freon cold storage refrigeration compressors.
  • Textiles, Steel & Stone Quarries: Automatic loom sheds, stone crushers, and steel rolling mills.
  • Paper Mills & Corrugated Packaging: Continuous hydrapulpers, paper machine drives, and corrugator line power distribution.
Why is it mandatory to hire a Govt. Authorized Electrical Contractor in Maharashtra? +

Under Indian Electricity Rules, only licensed electrical contractors authorized by the Maharashtra State Government Licensing Board (Industries, Energy & Labour Department) are legally permitted to execute, test, and certify HT/LT installations, substations, and passenger/freight lifts. Engaging an authorized contractor ensures full statutory compliance, insurance validity, and official acceptance of test certificates by utility authorities.

What is the procedure for obtaining D.G. Set (Diesel Generator) approval from the Electrical Inspector (PWD)? +

Obtaining statutory D.G. Set approval requires submitting an engineering Single Line Diagram (SLD) depicting the changeover switchgear, mechanical/electrical interlock verification to ensure no utility back-feeding, measurement of dedicated neutral and body earthing pits, acoustic enclosure noise compliance, and an on-site physical inspection by the Electrical Inspector (PWD). We prepare all engineering drawings, file statutory Form A and Form B, and secure final charging permission.

Do you handle High-Rise Building Electrical Permissions & Substation Clearances? +

Yes. Commercial and residential high-rise towers require specialized statutory scrutiny from the Electrical Inspectorate (Govt. of Maharashtra). We execute complete rising main busduct designs, D.G. set emergency changeovers, life-safety pump electrical panels, dual power source interlocks, and transformer substations, managing all PWD drawing approvals, on-site testing, and official final charging permissions.

How do you handle Electrical Inspector investigation clearances after a factory fire or electrical flashover? +

Following an industrial electrical fire or flashover, utility discoms cannot restore power without statutory clearance from the Electrical Inspector (Govt. of Maharashtra). Our senior engineers conduct emergency forensic damage assessments, perform Hi-Pot and Megger insulation resistance testing across cables and switchgear, draft remedial Single Line Diagrams (SLDs), rectify damaged distribution boards, and coordinate directly with the Electrical Inspectorate to obtain official inspection clearance and prompt power restoration.

Do you provide Emergency Transformer Rental Services for industrial plants? +

Yes. We provide emergency and temporary industrial transformer rentals (ranging from 100 kVA to 2000 kVA+) across Bhiwandi, Wada, Kudus, Thane, and Navi Mumbai. If your factory power transformer suffers an unexpected breakdown, winding flashover, or is undergoing major overhauls, our mobile emergency team swiftly delivers, mounts, cables, tests, and commissions a replacement rental transformer to eliminate manufacturing downtime.

What Transformer Testing and On-Site Vacuum Oil Filtration services do you offer? +

We provide complete NABL-traceable transformer diagnostic testing and servicing: Breakdown Voltage (BDV) dielectric oil strength test, Insulation Resistance (Megger 5kV/1kV), Winding Resistance, Voltage Ratio (Turns Ratio), Magnetic Balance, and Tan Delta / Capacitance testing. Our mobile high-vacuum oil filtration units perform continuous online/offline transformer oil heating, degassing, moisture extraction, and dehydration directly on-site to restore transformer dielectric strength to >60kV.

What HT Substation Testing and Switchgear Maintenance do you perform? +

Our licensed testing engineers carry out comprehensive preventive and statutory HT substation health audits: Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) & SF6 breaker contact resistance and closing/tripping time testing, numerical protection relay calibration (overcurrent, earth fault, differential), CT/PT ratio and knee-point excitation verification, HT cable DC Hi-Pot and surge testing, and calibrated 4-terminal Earth Megger resistance audits of neutral and body earthing grids.

Do you provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMCs) for industrial substations, transformers, and HT/LT panels? +

Yes. We offer both Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMCs) for continuous manufacturing plants, logistics hubs, and commercial complexes across Bhiwandi, Kudus, Wada, Thane, and Mumbai. Our AMC packages include:

  • HT Substation & Switchyard Servicing: Periodic inspection, busbar cleaning, insulator greasing, and VCB / SF6 breaker timing and contact resistance audits.
  • Transformer Health & Oil Conditioning: Regular BDV dielectric oil testing, moisture dehydration, silica gel replacement, and on-site high-vacuum oil filtration (>60 kV BDV).
  • LT Distribution & APFC Panel Maintenance: Thermal imaging of breaker terminations, contactor servicing, capacitor bank calibration to guarantee PF >0.98, and harmonic mitigation.
  • Protection Relay & Safety Calibration: Numerical overcurrent, earth fault, and master trip relay testing with calibrated secondary injection kits.
  • Earth Grid Safety Audits: Periodic 4-terminal Earth Megger resistance testing of neutral and body pits with PWD-compliant statutory maintenance logbooks.
  • 24x7 Emergency Breakdown Priority: Guaranteed rapid mobilization of senior engineers and emergency replacement transformers to avoid industrial downtime.

How do you help manufacturing plants avoid Low Power Factor (PF) penalties? +

Operating with a power factor below 0.90 attracts severe monthly penalties on utility bills from MSEDCL and Torrent Power, while maintaining a PF above 0.95 earns substantial incentives. We design, install, and calibrate Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) capacitor banks equipped with detuned harmonic filters to neutralize harmonics created by VFDs, extruders, and moulding machines, keeping your plant consistently at 0.99+ PF.
